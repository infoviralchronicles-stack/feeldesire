Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

At FeelDesire (accessible from feeldesire.com), the privacy and protection of our visitors are among our highest priorities. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document delineates the types of information collected and recorded by FeelDesire and precisely how we govern, process, and protect that data in accordance with international digital privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or interact with FeelDesire, we may gather certain categories of information to provide seamless editorial browsing experiences:

  • Log Files and Analytics: Like most standard web servers, FeelDesire makes use of log files. The information inside these files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is non-personally identifiable and is analyzed strictly to track visitor navigation, administer the site, and evaluate readership trends.
  • Cookies and Web Beacons: We utilize cookies to store information concerning visitor preferences, session personalization, and record user-specific page visits to customize our editorial content and elevate your browsing flow.
  • Direct Inquiries and Subscriptions: If you choose to contact us directly or subscribe to our FeelDesire weekly magazine newsletter, we collect the email address and contact information you volunteer in order to deliver pertinent responses and curated updates.

2. How We Utilize Your Information

FeelDesire applies collected data exclusively for legitimate journalistic, editorial, and platform operations, including:

  • Delivering, personalizing, and maintaining the functionality of our website and digital stories.
  • Analyzing user engagement to discover which technology, culture, and lifestyle topics resonate best with our audience.
  • Distributing weekly editorial digests and breaking news updates to opted-in subscribers.
  • Preventing malicious cyber activities, spam, and fraudulent interactions across our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookie Policies

Third-party ad networks, analytics engines (such as Google Analytics), and affiliate partners may deploy cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons within links displayed on FeelDesire. These automated scripts measure advertising campaign efficacy and adapt programmatic promotional experiences to your reading interests. FeelDesire maintains no direct control over external cookies operated by verified third-party partners.

You retain the unconditional right to disable cookies through your personal web browser settings. Comprehensive guides on cookie management are readily available via your respective web browser’s documentation.

4. Data Rights: GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We believe every reader deserves complete ownership of their digital footprint. Under statutory provisions, our global readership is entitled to:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request copies of personal data maintained in our newsletter records.
  • Right to Erasure & Rectification: You may instruct us to rectify inaccurate records or permanently purge your email address from our delivery systems at any juncture.
  • Right to Object & Opt-Out: Readers can immediately unsubscribe from any editorial newsletter through the single-click opt-out link embedded in every dispatch.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Protecting children online is an absolute imperative. FeelDesire does not knowingly harvest or solicit any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details through our platform, please get in touch with our team immediately, and we will expeditiously remove such records from our databases.

6. Inquiries and Contact Information

For inquiries, clarification, or data erasure requests concerning our privacy standards, please reach out to our legal and data compliance desk through our official Contact Us channel or via email at editorial@feeldesire.com.
