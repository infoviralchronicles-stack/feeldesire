Editorial Mission & Critical Approach

Alex Mercer has dedicated over eight years to cultural commentary, festival reporting, and digital entertainment economics. His investigative features explore the transition of classic cinematic conventions into interactive and high-fidelity streaming ecosystems. Mercer regularly interviews independent filmmakers, game designers, and cultural theorists to provide readers with nuanced perspectives on how digital entertainment continues to shape collective cultural memory.

His writing prioritizes analytical depth, ethical craftsmanship, and artistic intention over algorithm-driven hype, ensuring that FeelDesire readers receive discerning criticism and thoughtful cultural curation.

Accreditations & Professional Philosophy

Holding a master's degree in Media Studies and Cultural Anthropology from NYU Tisch School of the Arts, Alex has reported from major international film festivals including Sundance, Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF), and SXSW. His investigative journalism scrutinizes the evolving economics of digital subscription media, creator rights in streaming syndication, and how algorithmic personalization impacts narrative diversity in mainstream entertainment.

Mercer acts as a senior contributor to the FeelDesire Editorial Review Board, adhering strictly to the Society of Professional Journalists Code of Ethics. He lives in Brooklyn, New York, where he spends his weekends mentoring aspiring film critics and documenting historical cinema archives.
