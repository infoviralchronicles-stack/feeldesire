Who We Are

About FeelDesire

A premier digital publication exploring lifestyle, technology, culture, and wellness.

FeelDesire is an independent digital magazine devoted to uncovering the best ideas, products, and experiences shaping modern life. We blend astute investigative reporting with inspirational lifestyle curation for discerning readers worldwide.

Our Mission

At FeelDesire, our mission is to empower readers to live, work, and thrive with purpose. We cut through digital overwhelm to present authoritative reporting on breakthroughs in artificial intelligence, insightful architecture and smart home designs, transformative personal wellness habits, and the cultural milestones capturing global imagination.

What We Cover

Lifestyle & Living

Interior architecture, smart home transformations, restorative routines, and minimalist living.

Technology & Innovation

Autonomous tech, frontier AI, quantum breakthroughs, and hands-on product buyer guides.

Health & Wellness

Biohacking, holistic morning habits, sleep science, and evidence-based mental resilience.

Entertainment & Culture

Cinematic reviews, streaming cultural phenomena, and industry-defining creative projects.

Our Editorial Standards & Code of Ethics

Trust is the bedrock of FeelDesire. Our editors and contributors adhere to rigorous factual verification and journalistic balance. When we test a gadget, evaluate a mattress, or review a smartphone, we do so with hands-on rigor and complete editorial independence.

Sponsored features and commercial partnerships are consistently disclosed with full transparency to ensure our audience always knows where our editorial opinions stand.

Meet the Editorial Leadership

Our newsroom is powered by a diverse cadre of veteran journalists, subject-matter specialists, and digital creators stationed in key hubs across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific:

  • Emma Collins — Senior Lifestyle & Architecture Editor. Specializes in interior ergonomics and modern living rituals.
  • David Thorne — Technology & Digital Infrastructure Lead. Dedicated to frontier computing, mobile hardware, and the consumer AI boom.
  • Alex Mercer — Culture & Global Trends Correspondent. Covers cinematic milestones, entertainment business, and societal evolution.
