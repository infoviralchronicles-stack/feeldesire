Design Philosophy & Environmental Stewardship

Emma completed her architectural training at the Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) before pursuing specialized certifications in sustainable building materials and indoor air quality standards. Her writing challenges consumer culture's reliance on disposable seasonal decor, advocating instead for enduring heirloom furnishings, biophilic architectural integrations, and intentional restorative domestic environments that elevate mental health.

Beyond her writing desk, Emma conducts domestic ergonomic assessments for sustainable architecture firms across New England. Her research on daylighting strategies and acoustic dampening has been cited in prominent architectural design publications worldwide.
