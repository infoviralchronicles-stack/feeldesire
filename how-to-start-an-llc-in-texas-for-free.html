How to Start an LLC in Texas for Free: Step-by-Step 2026 Guide

By Alex Mercer • October 26, 2026

Launching a small business or side venture in the Lone Star State is one of the smartest economic decisions an entrepreneur can make. With zero state personal income tax, a booming enterprise ecosystem, and strong legal asset protection, Texas ranks as a nationwide powerhouse for startups. Yet when aspiring business owners search for how to start an LLC in Texas for free, they are immediately bombarded with aggressive advertisements from commercial formation websites promising "$0 LLC formation" before slapping them with hidden $299 upsells, recurring registered agent fees, and overpriced EIN registrations.

Here is the unfiltered reality: **you can legally form a Texas LLC for $0 out-of-pocket if you are an honorably discharged U.S. veteran**, thanks to statutory fee waiver programs enacted under Texas law. Furthermore, **if you are a non-veteran, you can eliminate 100% of third-party agency markup fees** by filing directly yourself with the Texas Secretary of State, serving as your own registered agent, and securing your federal EIN for free directly through the IRS.

Whether you qualify for the state statutory fee waiver or want to eliminate every unnecessary dollar of corporate legal middleman fees, this definitive step-by-step 2026 blueprint walks you through forming your Texas limited liability company with complete compliance and zero hidden markups.

The Truth About Free LLC Formation in Texas

Before submitting any paperwork, it is vital to distinguish between two distinct concepts: **State Statutory Fees** versus **Third-Party Service Markups**.

  • The True $0 State Waiver (Veterans): Under Texas Business Organizations Code § 3.005 and Senate Bill 938, businesses that are 100% owned by honorably discharged military veterans qualify for a complete waiver of the standard $300 Secretary of State Certificate of Formation filing fee. In addition, qualifying veteran-owned LLCs are exempt from the Texas state franchise tax for their first five years of operation.
  • The DIY Zero-Markup Route (Civilians): The state of Texas mandates a statutory $300 filing fee for civilian LLC registrations (Form 205). Commercial formation services advertise "free filing," but they charge $300 for the state fee plus exorbitant upsells for registered agent services ($150-$300/year), operating agreements ($99), and EIN processing ($75). By handling these simple administrative steps yourself directly through official state and federal portals, your service cost is exactly $0.

Step 1: Check and Reserve Your Texas LLC Name for Free

Every limited liability company in Texas must have a unique legal business name distinguishable from any other entity registered with the Texas Secretary of State. Under Texas law, your entity name must also contain an appropriate designator, such as "Limited Liability Company," "LLC," or "L.L.C."

To check name availability at zero cost without paying a legal service:

  1. Visit the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts Taxable Entity Search online portal. This database is completely free and publicly accessible.
  2. Enter your proposed business name to verify that no active corporation, LLC, or partnership is already utilizing the moniker.
  3. Ensure your name does not contain restricted terms (such as "Bank," "University," or "Trust") that require specialized regulatory approval.

Pro Tip: Do not pay a third party $40 to file a Texas Name Reservation. A name reservation is only necessary if you intend to delay forming your company for up to 120 days. If you are ready to file Form 205 immediately, submitting your formation paperwork automatically secures your legal business name upon approval.

Step 2: How Veterans Get the 100% Free Texas LLC Fee Waiver

If you or your co-founders are honorably discharged military veterans, Texas offers the most generous veteran entrepreneurship incentive program in the country. Follow these sequential steps to claim your $0 state filing:

1. Secure Your Veteran Verification Letter (VVL)

You cannot claim the waiver without formal state verification. Navigate to the Texas Veterans Commission (TVC) Veteran Entrepreneur Program portal (accessible through the Texas by Texas / TxT platform). Upload a copy of your military separation document (DD-214 Member 4 or Service 2 copy) demonstrating an honorable or general under honorable conditions discharge. The TVC will issue an official Veteran Verification Letter with a unique state tracking code.

2. Complete Comptroller Form 05-904

Download Form 05-904 (Certification of New Veteran-Owned Business) directly from the Texas Comptroller’s website. Ensure the business name on this form matches your intended LLC name character-for-character.

3. Submit via SOSUpload (Do Not Use Standard SOSDirect Checkout)

This is where most filers make a costly mistake: standard checkout on the automated SOSDirect portal automatically bills your credit card $300. Instead, package your completed Form 205 (Certificate of Formation), your Veteran Verification Letter, and Form 05-904 into clean PDF files. Upload the packet through the Secretary of State's **SOSUpload** system. State examiners manually verify your veteran documentation and process your LLC formation with a **$0 state fee**.

Step 3: Act as Your Own Texas Registered Agent for $0

Texas law requires every domestic LLC to continuously maintain a registered agent with a registered office in Texas. The registered agent is the designated person or entity responsible for accepting legal notices, tax correspondence, and service of process if your business is sued.

Commercial formation platforms frighten new entrepreneurs into paying $150 to $300 annually for commercial registered agent services. In reality, **any individual resident of Texas who is at least 18 years old and has a physical street address in the state can legally serve as their own registered agent for free**.

Requirements to Be Your Own Registered Agent:

  • You must have a physical address in Texas (a P.O. Box or virtual mail drop is legally invalid).
  • You must be physically present at the registered office during standard business hours (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) to accept legal correspondence.
  • You must sign a consent form (Form 401-A) to keep in your corporate records.

By serving as your own agent, you save thousands of dollars over the lifetime of your business while retaining direct control over all official state correspondence.

Step 4: Draft a Free Texas LLC Operating Agreement

An Operating Agreement is an internal legal contract governing the operational rules, ownership percentages, member voting rights, capital contributions, and profit distributions of your LLC. Commercial services charge $99 to $199 for this document, claiming it is required by law.

In Texas, the Secretary of State **does not require** you to submit or file an Operating Agreement with the state government. It is purely an internal governance document. However, creating one is strongly recommended because commercial banks and lending institutions require a copy before opening a business checking account.

Rather than paying an agency, you can draft an ironclad Operating Agreement for free using standard Texas statutory templates or customizable open-source business contract tools. Simply outline member names, management structure (Member-Managed vs. Manager-Managed), dispute resolution procedures, and dissolvement protocols, then have all members sign and store the agreement in your corporate binder.

Step 5: Get a Federal EIN from the IRS for 100% Free

An Employer Identification Number (EIN), also known as a Federal Tax Identification Number, is issued by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). Think of it as a corporate Social Security Number. You will need an EIN to open a dedicated business bank account, hire employees, establish commercial credit lines, and file federal tax returns.

Many deceptive third-party websites masquerade as government portals and charge unsuspecting founders $50 to $150 to obtain an EIN. **The IRS never charges for an EIN.**

To acquire your federal EIN instantly for $0:

  1. Navigate directly to the official IRS website (irs.gov) and search for the **Online EIN Assistant**.
  2. Select "Limited Liability Company" and input your newly approved Texas LLC legal name.
  3. Enter your name and Social Security Number as the responsible party.
  4. Submit the application during IRS online operating hours (Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM Eastern Time).
  5. Download and print your official IRS Confirmation Letter (Form CP 575) immediately upon completion.

Step 6: Texas Annual Reporting and Franchise Tax Requirements

Unlike states like California ($800 annual minimum tax) or Nevada ($350 annual list fee), Texas has an exceptionally friendly ongoing maintenance structure for small business owners.

Key annual maintenance facts for Texas LLCs:

  • No Annual Report Fee: Texas does not charge an annual renewal fee to keep your LLC in good standing.
  • Franchise Tax and Public Information Report (PIR): Every Texas LLC must file an annual Franchise Tax Report and Public Information Report with the Texas Comptroller by May 15 each year.
  • The No Tax Due Exemption: For the vast majority of small businesses and startups, your total annualized revenue will fall well beneath the statutory state threshold (which exceeds $2.47 million under current Texas statutes). If your revenue is below this threshold, you owe **$0 in state franchise tax**—you simply file a standardized "No Tax Due" information statement online via WebFile at zero cost.

Summary Checklist: Texas LLC for $0 Out-of-Pocket

  • Veteran LLC: $0 filing fee via TVC Veteran Verification Letter & SOSUpload.
  • Non-Veteran LLC: $300 statutory state fee, $0 legal agency service markups.
  • Registered Agent: $0 by serving as your own in-state agent.
  • EIN Tax ID: $0 directly on IRS.gov.
  • Operating Agreement: $0 using standard Texas corporate templates.
  • Annual State Franchise Fee: $0 under the No Tax Due threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you legally start an LLC in Texas for completely free?
Yes. Qualifying 100% veteran-owned businesses can legally start an LLC in Texas for $0 by securing a Veteran Verification Letter (VVL) and filing Comptroller Form 05-904 with Form 205 via SOSUpload. For non-veterans, you can form your LLC at $0 service fee by filing directly yourself with the Texas Secretary of State without paying third-party legal agencies $150 to $300 in predatory service charges.

What is the mandatory state filing fee for a Texas LLC?
For standard civilian filers, the Texas Secretary of State charges a statutory filing fee of $300 for Form 205 (Certificate of Formation). Qualified honorably discharged veterans receive a 100% statutory fee waiver, reducing this fee to $0.

Can I serve as my own registered agent in Texas for free?
Yes. Any Texas resident who is at least 18 years old and has a physical street address in Texas (not a P.O. Box) can serve as their own registered agent at zero cost, saving $100 to $300 annually in third-party registered agent fees.

How do I get a free EIN (Employer Identification Number) in Texas?
You can obtain an official federal EIN for 100% free directly on the IRS.gov website using the online EIN Assistant tool. It generates instantly upon application submission, and you should never pay a third-party service for this free federal form.

Does Texas have an annual LLC renewal fee?
Texas does not have an annual report fee. However, all Texas LLCs must file an annual Texas Franchise Tax Report and Public Information Report (PIR) with the Texas Comptroller by May 15 each year. If your annualized total revenue is below the statutory No Tax Due threshold, your state franchise tax liability is $0.
